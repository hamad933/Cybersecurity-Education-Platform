# Evidence, Mastery, and Review Interaction

Evidence cards show capability, origin, producer/run/attempt, criteria, digest, applicability, review decision, limitations, and date. Origin uses text plus icon; `SIMULATED` can never be styled as verified Real-Lab evidence.

Mastery presents the candidate state ladder and a chronological evidence/rule-decision ledger, not a progress percentage. A transition drawer lists rule-set revision, accepted/rejected evidence, unmet criteria, rationale, and reviewer. Provisional thresholds are labelled.

Review Queue groups by specific failure, missing evidence, prerequisite, or retention/transfer due date. Each item explains why selected and links to the relevant lesson block, practice, or lab case. Resolving a review creates new attempt/evidence; it does not erase the failure.
