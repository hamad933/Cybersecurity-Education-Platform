# Product Definition Summary

The candidate defines an independent, Arabic-first, local-first Cybersecurity Learning and Operations Simulation Platform for one local owner. It unifies governed source evidence, canonical revisioned knowledge, capability-linked curriculum, adaptive learning, institutional simulation, enterprise/scenario modeling, evidence-based mastery, portfolio, and a human-controlled Manual AI Bridge.

The owner may act as learner, author, scenario designer, curriculum reviewer, or professional-workflow reviewer, but simulated roles never grant application authority. v1 completeness is workflow completeness proven through bounded vertical slices and recovery/security checks; it is not full content across 16 Domains and it is not a claim of competence.

The platform is not CARE, a conventional LMS, a notes app, a static question bank, a production security tool, an offensive execution platform, or a replacement for authorized real environments. Core v1 is useful without public Internet. Automated/provider/local AI and real-execution connectors are excluded.
