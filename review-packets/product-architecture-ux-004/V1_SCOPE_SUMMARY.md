# v1 Scope Summary

The scope matrix contains 19 `IN_V1`, 6 `OUTSIDE_V1`, and 2 `ROADMAP_EXCLUDED` items.

In v1: one local owner; governed source custody, safe import/extraction states, authority and provenance; canonical/revisioned knowledge; capability-linked curriculum; adaptive lifecycle and evidence-based mastery; persistent enterprise catalog; Scenario Studio and deterministic simulator; evidence/portfolio; Manual AI Bridge only; search/queue, backup/restore, audit, export, security and accessibility baselines; and three bounded vertical slices plus hardening.

Post-v1 or deferred: broad curriculum content, controlled multi-user/collaboration, optional PWA, measured specialized workers, advanced search infrastructure only if PostgreSQL is proven insufficient, and selectively authorized claim-specific real-lab paths.

Roadmap-excluded: automated/provider/local AI and generalized provider frameworks; SaaS/marketplace and real-execution or offensive connectors. Product completeness never means complete 16-Domain curriculum.
