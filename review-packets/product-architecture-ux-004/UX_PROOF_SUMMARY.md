# UX Proof Summary

The local proof demonstrates one product shell with eight distinct workspaces rather than one giant page: dashboard, source review, Knowledge Studio, curriculum/capability map, Guided Lab, Enterprise Designer/Scenario Studio, evidence/mastery, and Manual AI Bridge.

It uses Arabic-first RTL layout with isolated LTR identifiers, commands, code, paths, and hashes. It represents draft, published, simulated, imported, review-required, blocked, and unresolved states in text. Responsive navigation, a lesson disclosure, a simulated lab result, and keyboard focus operate locally and ephemerally. The proof loads no external asset and executes no business workflow.

Fourteen rendered PNGs cover all eight desktop views, three mobile states, two mixed-direction close-ups, and visible keyboard focus. This is design evidence only, not a product or a WCAG/usability certification.
